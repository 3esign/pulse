01How It's Played

Two ways to earn. Both pay straight into your wallet, on-chain, the moment they trigger.

🏁 The launch race

The first 10 wallets to buy PULSE claim a rank — and an instant SOL payout straight from the game pot. Rank #1 gets the biggest slice, rank #10 the smallest. It decays fast and there's no second chance once the 10 ranks are gone.

💚 The pulse

Once the launch race is full, every trade — buy or sell — keeps the pulse alive and earns the trader a reward from the pot. Flip a trade (buy then sell) and you earn a bonus on top. Active trading is the whole game.

02How It Actually Works

Everything below is independently verifiable on-chain — that's the entire point of building it this way.

Why pump.fun

PULSE's token launch and its game are the same event. pump.fun tokens trade on a bonding curve — price is a deterministic function of SOL in, no liquidity pool needed up front — and every trade generates a small creator fee. That fee is PULSE's entire funding source: no treasury, no team allocation, no VC unlock schedule. This launch splits every creator fee 66.33% into the game pot, 33.67% to the project — decided once, on-chain, and irreversible from the moment the split is set.

The on-chain program

PULSE's game logic is a custom Solana program built on Pinocchio, a minimal low-level framework that produces a far smaller compiled binary than typical alternatives — real savings, since every byte of a deployed program is backed by a permanent, real rent deposit. It exposes three instructions:

  • init_counter — one-time setup for the launch-race counter.
  • claim_rank — callable while ranks 1–10 are open; verifies the caller holds the real PULSE mint (hardcoded at compile time, not passed in at runtime), assigns the next rank, and pays out immediately.
  • record_activity — callable on every trade once the race is full; verifies the trade, credits activity, and pays the reward (plus flip bonus) straight from the pot.

Every payout is a direct lamport transfer from a program-owned PDA (Program-Derived Address — an address computed from the program's own ID, held by no one's private key). There is no admin instruction to drain the pot, pause payouts, or change the rules after deployment.

Permissionless by design

Any wallet holding the real PULSE mint can call claim_rank or record_activity directly — no allowlist, no backend approval, no API key. The program's on-chain bytecode is the only authority, and anyone can inspect it. That openness is a deliberate v1 tradeoff: reward sizes are kept modest specifically because a fully open, low-friction mechanism is easier to interact with at scale than a gated one — an economic risk accepted on purpose, not an oversight.

Real mainnet deployment

PULSE's program was deployed to Solana mainnet with the standard CLI (solana program deploy), funded with exactly enough SOL to cover the network's rent-exemption requirement for its compiled size — a permanent, refundable-only-by-deletion deposit sized to the program's byte length, not a fee that disappears. Every account this game touches — the pot, the launch-race counter, the program itself — is a real mainnet account with a real, checkable balance.
